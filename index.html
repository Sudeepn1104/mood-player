1. Import from Instagram

  1. Instagram → your profile → ☰ → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  2. Choose Some of your information → tick Saved → Download to device, Format JSON, Date range All time.
  3. When Instagram emails you, download the zip and extract it (Files app → tap zip → Extract).
  4. Pick the file saved_collections.json below (it's in your_instagram_activity/saved).
